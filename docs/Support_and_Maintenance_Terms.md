3. **Data Import / Export Troubleshooting:** Support in validating Excel (.xlsx) and CSV templates, resolving duplicate family conflicts, and exporting printable PDF reports.
4. **License Management & Machine Transfer:** Assistance with commercial license key activations, certificate exports, and hardware replacement key transfers.
5. **Defect Rectification & Maintenance Releases:** Providing software updates and bug fixes for verified application defects.

### 3. SERVICES EXCLUDED (OUT OF SCOPE)
1. **Official TTD Operations:** Quota availability, booking dates, VIP letter approval, and Darshan scheduling are governed exclusively by Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams. The vendor has no influence over TTD portal operations.
2. **Payment & Banking Issues:** Resolution of payment gateway declines, bank server timeouts, or OTP delivery delays.
3. **Hardware / OS Repairs:** Fixing physical PC hardware failure, Windows malware infections, or corrupt operating system installations.
