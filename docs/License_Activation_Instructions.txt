SRIVANI BOOKING MANAGER v5.0.0 — LICENSE ACTIVATION & RECOVERY INSTRUCTIONS
================================================================================

1. OFFLINE ACTIVATION STEPS
All license verification in SRIVANI BOOKING MANAGER is 100% offline.
No internet connection is required to validate or activate your software.

To activate your commercial license:
1. Launch SRIVANI BOOKING MANAGER on your Windows computer.
2. If this is a fresh installation, the First-Run Setup Wizard opens automatically.
   If already installed, click "Preferences & About" (gear icon) -> "Client Licensing".
3. Locate your unique 16-character Machine Installation ID:
   Example: SRVN-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX
   Click "Copy ID" to copy it to your clipboard.
4. Provide your Installation ID and Organization Name to Devotee Technologies
   or your authorized software distributor.
5. In return, you will receive your signed 24-character Commercial License Key:
   Example: SRVN-P3CB-4A52-4F38-6D34
6. Enter your Client Name and the License Key into the activation dialog.
7. Click "Activate License".
8. A green badge will confirm: "License State: ACTIVE (COMMERCIAL)".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. 30-DAY FREE EVALUATION MODE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If you do not yet have a commercial license key:
1. Open the First-Run Setup Wizard or go to Preferences -> Client Licensing.
2. Select "Start 30-Day Evaluation License".
3. All features, queues, Excel/CSV tools, and reports are immediately unlocked
   for 30 days without restriction.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. LICENSE RECOVERY PROCEDURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If you reinstall Windows, format your drive, or migrate computers:
1. Re-install SRIVANI BOOKING MANAGER on your computer.
2. If reinstalling on the SAME computer, your Machine Installation ID remains
   identical. Simply re-enter your original Commercial License Key to restore
   full commercial activation immediately.
3. If migrating to a NEW computer, your Machine Installation ID will change.
   Send your new Installation ID along with proof of purchase to support for
   a free license re-issuance.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. GRACE PERIOD / SAFE MODE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If your evaluation license reaches its expiration date:
- The software DOES NOT delete records, block access, or lock your databases.
- You can continue viewing all families, pilgrims, audit logs, and running backups.
- You can activate a commercial license key at any time to resume full access.
