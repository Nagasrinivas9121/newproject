### 1. GRANT AND ACTIVATION
1.1 **License Model:** Single-installation, machine-bound commercial desktop license.  
1.2 **Hardware Binding:** The software license is tied to an Installation ID derived from the hardware environment (`SRVN-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`).  
1.3 **Offline Verification:** Activation is verified cryptographically via an offline HMAC-SHA256 key (`SRVN-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`). No internet connection is required or established for license verification.  
1.4 **Multi-Seat Licenses:** Where specifically licensed as a site or multi-seat deployment, vendor may provide a site-wide key valid across authorized client workstations.

---

### 2. EVALUATION & EXPIRATION POLICY
2.1 **Evaluation Mode:** New installations may be evaluated for up to thirty (30) calendar days from initialization.  
2.2 **Advance Expiry Notice:** Transparent visual alerts appear within the application when fourteen (14) or fewer days remain on an evaluation or term license.  
2.3 **Non-Destructive Post-Expiry Behavior:** When a license reaches its expiration date:
   - The application switches to an evaluation grace mode.
   - All previously entered donor rosters, family queue records, and ticket files remain accessible.
   - No data is wiped, truncated, encrypted, or destroyed.
   - Client may activate a renewed commercial key at any time.

### 4. RECOVERY AND HARDWARE TRANSFER
4.1 **Right of Transfer:** Client retains the right to transfer the license to a replacement computer in the event of hardware upgrades or repairs.  
4.2 **Procedure:** Client must execute the in-app "Deactivate for Transfer" procedure to obtain a signed Deactivation Proof code, or present their exported License Certificate to vendor support.  
4.3 **Re-Issuance:** Vendor will issue a replacement license key for the new computer's Installation ID without additional fee during an active support term.
