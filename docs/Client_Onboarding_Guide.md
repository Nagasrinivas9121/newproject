# FIRST-TIME CLIENT ONBOARDING GUIDE

## Welcome to SRIVANI Booking Manager
This guide walks administrative operators through their first 30 minutes with the software, ensuring a smooth, verified transition to production operations.

---

### Step 1: Pre-Installation Check
1. Ensure the workstation meets minimum requirements (Windows 10/11 64-bit, 4 GB RAM, 500 MB disk).
2. Ensure you have administrator rights on the PC to run the installer.
3. Keep your commercial license key or order receipt handy.

---

### Step 2: Running Setup & First-Run Wizard
1. Launch `SrivaniBookingManager_Setup_v6.0.0.exe` and complete installation.
2. Launch **SRIVANI Booking Manager** from the new desktop shortcut.
3. The **First-Run Setup Wizard** will start automatically:
   - Accept the license agreement.
   - Enter your commercial license key (or choose the 30-day evaluation mode).
   - Create your master administrator account with a strong password.
   - Confirm storage folders.
   - Click **Complete Setup & Launch**.

---

### Step 3: Verifying System Health
1. Log in with your new administrator username and password.
2. Click **⚙️ Preferences & About** on the bottom-left sidebar.
3. Under the **ℹ️ About & Version** tab, click **🔍 Run Environment Audit**.
4. Confirm all checks show green `[PASS]`.

---

### Step 4: Loading Initial Devotee Data
1. Prepare your existing family donor list using the provided `sample_families.xlsx` or `sample_families.csv` template.
2. In the application, navigate to **📥 Import & Export**.
3. Select your spreadsheet file and click **Preview Records**.
4. Verify the preview columns: Family Name, Devotee Count, Phone Number, Preferred Date.
5. Click **Confirm & Import**. The families will immediately populate your **📊 Dashboard & Queue**.

---

### Step 5: Test Booking Workspace Workflow
1. Select the first family in the queue and click **⚡ Family Workspace**.
2. Click **🌐 Open TTD Portal** to verify that your default web browser opens the official TTD website (`https://ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in`).
3. Practice using the **Copy** buttons next to devotee names and Aadhaar numbers.
4. Try updating the family's status to **In progress** and observe the real-time queue badge update.

---

### Step 6: Create First Database Backup
1. Navigate to **💾 Backup & Recovery** in the sidebar.
2. Click **💾 Create Backup Now**.
3. Verify that the backup file appears in the table with an `[OK / Valid]` integrity status.
4. Your onboarding is complete and your system is fully production-ready!
